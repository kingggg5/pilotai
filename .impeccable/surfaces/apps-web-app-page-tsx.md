## Scope and mode

Primary route `/` (`apps/web/app/page.tsx`). Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

## Audience, job, and action

Thai- or English-speaking customers need to understand the featured product, see an honest price reference, and add it to a lightweight cart without entering staff operations. The primary action is to add iPhone 17 Pro Max to the cart; authenticated support is secondary.

## Proof and constraints

The current item is iPhone 17 Pro Max 256GB in Deep Blue at ฿48,900, checked against Apple Thailand on 14 August 2026. ServicePilot is collecting purchase requests, not claiming an immediate payment transaction or authorized-reseller status. Cart data remains browser-local. Support ticket creation requires a verified customer session.

## Chosen direction

**The Product Current.** Extend the Proof Studio with one deep-blue product field contained inside the storefront hero. The device study is drawn from native CSS shapes, while black, paper, and lime retain all navigation and state semantics. A continuous vertical sequence moves from product promise to key facts and provenance without decorative card grids.

## Unresolved decisions

Production catalog inventory, taxes, shipping, payment provider, order persistence, returns, and reseller/merchant-of-record status require business decisions before ServicePilot can offer transactional checkout.
